Storm Readiness & Post-Storm Property Checks

We help you prepare your home before severe weather and provide timely inspections after storms so you can quickly understand your property's condition.

peace of mind

Fast Response When It Matters Most

Storm damage can worsen quickly if not identified early. Having a local team check your property immediately after a storm provides peace of mind and a faster response — especially when you're hours away.

Local Luxe Concierge is your eyes and hands on the ground. We document conditions before the storm and inspect for damage immediately after, giving you a clear picture of your property's status and helping you coordinate any needed repairs quickly.

our approach

Before & After the Storm

We provide comprehensive coverage on both ends of a weather event, giving you the full picture of your property's condition.

Pre-Storm Support
  • ✦ Move outdoor furniture, grills, plants, and decor to reduce wind damage risk
  • ✦ Identify visible vulnerabilities before the storm arrives
  • ✦ Verify windows and doors are properly secured
  • ✦ Ensure the property is prepared as best as possible before the weather event
Post-Storm Checks
  • ✦ Exterior walkaround to identify visible damage and debris
  • ✦ Interior inspection for leaks, water intrusion, or structural concerns
  • ✦ Photo documentation of all findings for insurance purposes
  • ✦ Clear, timely communication of findings so you can act quickly
why it matters

Storm Damage Doesn't Wait

Early Detection Saves Money

A small roof leak identified within hours of a storm can be managed for hundreds of dollars. Left undetected for days, it can mean tens of thousands in water and mold damage.

Documentation for Insurance Claims

Time-stamped photos from our post-storm inspection provide the clear record your insurance company needs to process claims quickly and accurately.

Faster Contractor Response

After major storms, contractors are in high demand. Our local relationships and immediate assessment help you get ahead of the queue for repairs.

Local Eyes, Immediate Action

We're already here. No flight to book, no delayed response. We can be at your property within hours of a storm clearing to give you a full status report.
