Local Care for Your Coastal Property

Owning a vacation property in the Lowcountry means enjoying one of the most beautiful regions in the country — but managing it from afar presents real challenges. Local Luxe Concierge acts as your trusted local representative, coordinating everything your property needs between your visits.

what we provide

Vacation Home Concierge Services

From pre-arrival preparation to property oversight between visits, we handle the details so every trip to your Lowcountry home is exactly what you want it to be.

Pre-Arrival Preparation

We prepare your home before you arrive — turning on utilities, adjusting climate settings, restocking essentials, and ensuring everything is clean and guest-ready.

Property Condition Inspections

Regular visual inspections with written reports and time-stamped photos so you always know the exact condition of your property between visits.

Grocery & Essentials Stocking

We'll shop and stock your home with groceries, household essentials, and anything else you need so you can settle in immediately upon arrival.

Vendor & Maintenance Coordination

We schedule, meet, and oversee cleaning crews, lawn care, pool service, and any contractors who need property access — so you never have to coordinate from afar.

Property Security Oversight

Routine security checks, alarm system verification, and monitoring of all entry points — keeping your property secure during extended absences.

Departure Checklist

After your stay, we secure the home — locking up, adjusting climate controls, running through your departure checklist, and ensuring everything is in order until next time.

Emergency Response

When urgent issues arise — a burst pipe, HVAC failure, or storm damage — we respond immediately and coordinate the appropriate professionals on your behalf.

Experience Reservations

Restaurant reservations, tee times, activity bookings, and curated local recommendations — so every visit to the Lowcountry is extraordinary from the moment you arrive.

a note on rental management

Our complete focus is on you, the homeowner, and ensuring your property receives the highest standard of care on your behalf.
